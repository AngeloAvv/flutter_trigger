title: "Activation milestone"
description: "Celebrate the aha moment, and use it to gate later prompts."
---

## Goal

Define the moment a user "gets" the product — the activation milestone — celebrate it, and use it as
a prerequisite for prompts that only make sense afterwards (ratings, referrals, upsells).

## The rule

Pick a count and a spread over time from your own retention data. Here: ten completed tasks over at
least three different days.

Famous examples are correlations found in one product's data, and some are less documented than
their fame suggests:

- **Slack**: teams that exchanged 2,000 messages had "really tried" Slack, and 93% of them were still
  using it — Stewart Butterfield on First Round Review.
- **Twitter**: users who followed about 30 accounts became long-term users, per Josh Elman's analysis
  reported in _Hacking Growth_ — with the authors' own caveat that correlation is not causation.
- **Facebook's "7 friends in 10 days"** is widely quoted, but no primary source states it precisely;
  Andrew Chen notes the exact numbers matter less than having a simple goal.

## Code

```dart
void registerActivation(Nudge nudge) {
  nudge
      .define('activation_celebration')
      .when(
        nudge
            .when()
            .event('task_completed')
            .count()
            .greaterThanOrEqual(10)
            .and(nudge.when().app().activeDays().greaterThanOrEqual(3)),
      )
      .maxFires(1)
      .register();
}
```

Gate other nudges on the milestone by reusing the condition, or on the celebration having fired:

```dart
final activated = nudge.when().fired('activation_celebration').exists();

nudge
    .define('referral_prompt')
    .when(activated.and(referralCondition))
    .register();
```
