title: "Streaks and habits"
description: "Celebrate streak milestones and warn when a streak is at risk, Duolingo-style."
---

## Goal

Build a daily habit: celebrate streak milestones and warn the user when today's practice is missing.

## The rule

- Streak: consecutive local days with at least one completed lesson
- Milestones at 3, 7 and 30 days, each celebrated once
- At risk: a streak of two days or more, and no lesson yet today

Duolingo reports that learners who reach a 7-day streak are 3.6 times more likely to complete their
course, and that the first days of a streak are the most fragile.

## Code

```dart
void registerStreaks(Nudge nudge) {
  final lessons = nudge.when().event('lesson_completed');

  for (final days in [3, 7, 30]) {
    nudge
        .define('streak_milestone_$days')
        .when(lessons.streak().greaterThanOrEqual(days))
        .maxFires(1)
        .register();
  }

  nudge
      .define('streak_at_risk')
      .when(
        lessons
            .streak()
            .greaterThanOrEqual(2)
            .and(lessons.today().exists().not()),
      )
      .oncePerSession()
      .cooldown(const Duration(hours: 12))
      .register();
}
```

Show the streak itself with a `NudgeBuilder`, or read it in an explanation:

```dart
NudgeBuilder(
  condition: nudge.when().event('lesson_completed').streak().greaterThanOrEqual(1),
  builder: (context, onStreak) => StreakFlame(lit: onStreak),
)
```

## Pitfalls

- A streak survives until a full local day passes without the event, so "at risk" is a matter of
  hours: show it when the user opens the app in the evening, and schedule a local notification for
  users who don't.
- Days follow the device's local midnight: travelling across time zones can shorten or lengthen a
  day. Duolingo offers streak freezes for exactly this kind of friction.
